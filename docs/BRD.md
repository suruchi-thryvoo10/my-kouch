## 2. Executive Summary
The project involves creating a premium sofa and furniture website for MyKouch, a company specializing primarily in sofas and couches. The business operates on an enquiry-based sales model rather than a traditional e-commerce direct checkout flow. 

The website will serve as a digital catalog, allowing customers to professionally discover sofas, explore collections, view detailed product information, and select available configurations. Instead of purchasing directly, customers will submit an enquiry or contact the sales team via WhatsApp, phone, or an online form. Following the enquiry, the sales team will handle the customer conversation, provide quotations, and manage the payment process. 

---

## 3. Business Background
MyKouch is a premium furniture business offering a variety of seating products. 
- **Primary product:** Sofas and couches (including L-Shape, Combos, and Recliners).
- **Sales model:** Enquiry-based (no direct online add-to-cart/checkout for immediate purchase).
- **Salesperson involvement:** Required immediately following an online enquiry.
- **Quotation:** Provided directly by a salesperson after initial contact.
- **Payment:** Handled post-quotation (may be online or offline, requiring further confirmation).

A dedicated online presence is required to showcase the company's offerings professionally, attract potential buyers, and generate qualified leads for the sales team. The website acts as the primary digital touchpoint supporting the sales team by facilitating discovery and structured enquiries.

## 4. Business Objectives
- Establish a strong, professional online presence for MyKouch.
- Present the sofa collection professionally to reflect the brand's premium nature.
- Make it easy for customers to discover products and explore collections.

## 7. Target Users

### Customer
- **Who they are:** Individuals or businesses looking for premium sofas, couches, and recliners.
- **What they need:** A trustworthy platform to explore designs, dimensions, and configurations of furniture.
- **What they should be able to accomplish:** Browse the catalogue, find detailed specifications, configure options, and easily send an enquiry or contact a salesperson for pricing and purchase.

### Salesperson

## 14. Non-Functional Business Requirements

### User Experience
- **Premium:** The visual design must reflect the high quality of the furniture.
- **Professional:** Clean layout, high-quality imagery, and clear typography.
- **Easy to navigate:** Intuitive menus (Home, Sofas, Offers, Contact).
- **Mobile-friendly:** Must work flawlessly on smartphones.
- **Responsive:** Adapts to all screen sizes (tablet, desktop, etc.).

### Performance
- **Fast product/image loading:** Images must load quickly to maintain a premium feel.
- **Smooth browsing experience:** Seamless transitions between product discovery and enquiry.

### Accessibility
- **Readable content:** High contrast text and legible font sizes.
- **Clear buttons:** Obvious, tappable calls-to-action (e.g., "ENQUIRE").
- **Usable on different screen sizes:** Scalable UI elements.

### Trust
- **Clear company information:** Professional branding.
- **Contact information:** Easily accessible phone and WhatsApp links.
- **Product details:** Accurate specifications to set correct expectations.
- **Professional visual presentation:** Consistency in image styles and branding.

## 18. Risks

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation / Action Required |
|------|------------------|------------------------------|
| Incomplete product information | Poor customer experience and low trust | Client must provide a complete data template before launch |
| Inconsistent product images | Website looks unprofessional, undermining "premium" brand | Establish image guidelines (aspect ratio, background) for client |
| Unclear pricing strategy | Confusion over whether prices should be shown or hidden | Client must confirm if prices are public or "Price on Request" |
| Unclear configuration options | Customers cannot accurately request what they want | Client must map all possible configurations per product |
| Delayed enquiry handling | Loss of potential sales | Define expected SLA for salesperson follow-up |
| Unclear quotation workflow | Friction between sales and customer | Client to define exact quotation handover process |
| Unclear payment process | Ambiguity at the close of sale | Client to clarify online vs. offline payment procedures |

## 20. Open Questions / Client Confirmation Required

### Products
- How many sofas/products are there in total?
- Are there categories other than L-Shape, Combos, and Recliners?
- What specific configurations are available (e.g., left-arm vs right-arm, size variants)?
- Can customers completely customize sofas?
- What materials are available?
- What colours are available?
- Are exact dimensions available for all products?
- Is pricing going to be shown publicly on the website, or is it strictly "Price on Request"?

### Sales
- Is every enquiry handled manually by a human?
- Who specifically receives the enquiries (a shared inbox, individual sales reps)?
- How quickly should enquiries be followed up (SLA)?
- How is the quotation created (e.g., PDF, email text, accounting software)?
- Is any part of the quotation generated automatically through the website?
- Is payment handled entirely offline, or do we need to facilitate online payments via a link later?
- If online payment is required, at what specific stage does it occur?

### Website
- Is a customer login portal required to track their enquiries?
- Is a search bar required?
- Are product filters required (e.g., filter by color, material)?
- Is a wishlist required?
- Is product comparison required?
- Are customer reviews required to be displayed?
- What is the expected flow for the "Offers" and "Become a Dealer" sections?

### Admin
- Who manages products?
- Who manages enquiries?
- Who can change product information?
- Who can upload images?

### Business
- What are the supported delivery locations?
- Are there delivery charges?
- Is installation included?
- What is the warranty policy?
- What is the return/cancellation policy?
- Do you accept custom design orders outside of the catalogue?
- What is the typical lead time from enquiry to delivery?

### Brand
- What are the exact brand colours (hex codes)?
- What fonts should be used?
- Do you have high-res variations of the logo?
- What is the brand story (for "About Us")?
- What are the official social media links?
- What are the official contact details?
